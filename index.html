The Space

Bakery by day, kitchen by night

Warm brass signage, dark wood beams and low amber light set the tone the moment you walk in. PannaKotta runs as a bakery counter up front and a full kitchen behind it — pastries and fresh bread on one side, pasta, pizza, burgers and subs on the other.

Everything on the savoury side is vegetarian, with a dedicated Jain menu for guests who need it, and the whole menu is available for dine-in or parcel.
